The creator

Shreyash is the developer and creator of ROOM/05. The project reflects an interest in building interactive web experiences and experimenting with real-time browser technology.

The project

ROOM/05 brings together private rooms, peer-to-peer audio and video, chat, shared theater experiences and lightweight social reactions in one browser-based space.
