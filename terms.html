1. Agreement

By selecting the agreement option and using ROOM/05, you confirm that you have read and agree to these Terms & Conditions and the Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not create an account or use the service.

Use ROOM/05 only where you are legally allowed to do so. If local law requires permission from a parent or guardian, obtain that permission before using the service.

2. Accounts and passwords

You may create an account with a username and password. A permanent ROOM/05 user ID is generated for the account. Usernames must be unique and must not impersonate another person or brand.

Passwords are stored as salted, one-way password hashes on the ROOM/05 server; the application is not designed to store plain-text passwords. You are responsible for keeping your password private and for activity performed through your account.

ROOM/05 does not currently provide email verification, password reset or account recovery. Keep your password safe. If you forget it, the account may become inaccessible. Do not reuse a password that protects another important account.

3. Community rules

Do not use ROOM/05 to threaten, bully, harass, stalk, impersonate, defraud, exploit or discriminate against anyone. Do not share another person's private information without permission. Do not upload, transmit or display unlawful, abusive or otherwise harmful content.

Do not interfere with the website, attempt to access accounts or rooms without permission, probe or disrupt the server, or bypass technical limits.

4. Rooms and media

Rooms are private by invite code, not by a guarantee that every participant is trustworthy. Anyone who obtains a valid room code may be able to join while a seat is available. Share codes carefully and check the participant list.

Camera, microphone and screen sharing are controlled by your browser and device permissions. Audio and video are designed to travel directly between participants using WebRTC. Do not share content that you do not have permission to show. Other participants may capture audio or video using their own devices even though ROOM/05 does not provide a recording feature.

5. Privacy

The Privacy Policy explains what account information, technical information and room data the service handles, where account records are stored, and what the hosting provider may process. It forms part of these terms.

6. Availability and limitations

ROOM/05 is provided on an as-available basis. Calls may be interrupted by network conditions, device compatibility, browser permissions, hosting maintenance or service limits. Peer-to-peer calls depend on participant upload speeds and network configurations.

The service has a maximum of five participants per room in its standard configuration. Features such as screen sharing may not work in every mobile browser.

7. Safety and responsibility

Use ROOM/05 thoughtfully. Do not share passwords, payment details, private documents, precise location information or other sensitive information in a room or chat. Leave a room if you feel uncomfortable and tell a trusted adult about concerning behaviour.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, the project operator is not responsible for user-generated content, other participants' actions, network failures or loss of access to an account caused by a forgotten password.

8. Changes and termination

These terms or the service may change as ROOM/05 evolves. The version and date above identify the current published terms. Continued use after updated terms are presented means you accept the updated terms.

The project operator may suspend or restrict access where reasonably necessary to protect users, the service or comply with law. You may stop using ROOM/05 at any time.
